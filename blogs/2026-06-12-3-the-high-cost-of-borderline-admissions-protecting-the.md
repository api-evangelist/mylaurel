Every health system CEO and CFO knows that the Emergency Department is the front door of the organization. What is less discussed, however, is how easily that front door can dilute your inpatient margin when lower-acuity or borderline patients are admitted simply because there is no viable downstream alternative. When an ED is packed, and a complex, elderly, or chronic patient presents with borderline criteria, the safest default for the ED physician is often admission.
