In our last post, we established that shaving days off an inpatient's length of stay (LOS) rarely results in actual labor savings due to rigid, stepped staffing ratios. Today, let’s look at the hard math that proves why a backfill strategy is the single fastest way to repair a compromised operating margin. When a hospital reduces its LOS, the traditional finance team multiplies those saved days by the marginal cost per day (variable labor, food, linens, variable pharmacy costs) to report savings.
