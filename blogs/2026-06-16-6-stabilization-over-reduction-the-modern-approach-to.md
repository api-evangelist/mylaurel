In the current healthcare environment, traditional productivity metrics can be dangerous. If a CFO demands a blanket reduction in full-time equivalents (FTEs) to meet a budget target, the result is almost always increased staff burnout, a spike in costly turnovers, and a subsequent reliance on premium agency labor. True operational efficiency today is not about cutting staff; it is about stabilizing your current workforce and maximizing their capacity by eliminating systemic operational friction.
