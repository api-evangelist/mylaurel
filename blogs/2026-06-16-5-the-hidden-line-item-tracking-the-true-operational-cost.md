If you review your monthly financial variances and see unbudgeted spikes in contract labor, premium overtime, and revenue leakage, you don’t necessarily have a scheduling problem. You likely have an ED boarding problem. ED boarding—holding admitted inpatients in emergency bays because there are no clean upstairs beds available—is often viewed as an operational nuisance or a patient satisfaction issue.
